There is a strange relationship between the speed of a website and the quality of attention it invites.

When a page takes five seconds to load—jerking back and forth as banner ads shift the layout and popups demand compliance—your brain enters an agitated state before you read a single sentence.

## The cp-algorithms inspiration

One website I have always admired is [cp-algorithms.com](https://cp-algorithms.com/). 

It doesn't try to impress you with 50-megabyte hero videos or complex animations that hijack your trackpad. Instead, it does three things exceptionally well:

1. **Instant responsiveness:** You click a link, and the content appears before your finger leaves the mouse.
2. **Crystal-clear hierarchy:** A clean sidebar on the left, an unobtrusive table of contents on the right, and the article right in the center.
3. **Respect for reading:** Good font sizes, pleasant contrast, and zero distractions.

## Building for focus

When designing this site, that was the gold standard. We utilized **Astro** and native **View Transitions** so moving between thoughts feels smooth and continuous, like flipping pages in a well-bound notebook.

When the machinery of the web gets out of the way, all that remains is the thought itself.
