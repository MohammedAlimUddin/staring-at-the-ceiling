# staring at the ceiling

I am being restless, tired then restless again. I'm being that slave of the race master, world order or whatever day by day. And it hurts. If one thing in the world that I fear is- not being me. And the world around is pushing me in that way. It's scary. And I'm scared. I know it is inevitable to escape. But least I can do is renting about it. That's why this blog exist. 

I sometimes stare at the ceiling and let my inner 'me' have a conversation with himself. Sometimes he sounds stupid, sometimes smart, sometimes he behaves like an annoying freak. But I do enjoy listening to him. But my never settling consciousness is unable to remember all that. I feel like letting them fade away is a big miss. Not for anybody else. But for me. You see, I like being nostalgic. God, I love being nostalgic. Probably one of my most favourite pastimes. People or events are great source of nostalgia. I get that and all. But probably the most chilling source of nostalgia is past version of oneself. Thinking about what I was doing at a particular day, how the day was always sends chills down my spine. Maybe I'm a bit self-centered. Maybe that's normal. I don't care. 

So, anyway, thats why I decided to create a blog, where I will dump whatever silly thoughts I have (I decided it while staring at the ceiling, ofcourse). 

I'll not use any in AI writing, proof reading or editing.
